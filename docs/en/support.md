# Support & Services

DB Manager is an Apache-2.0 open-source project — free to use, modify, and redistribute
without restriction. The options below are for those who want to support the project's
continued development. You are buying **service, not software** — without sponsoring or
subscribing, no feature is withheld.

## Sponsorship

The Sponsor button on the repository page (GitHub Sponsors) supports one-time and monthly
donations. Any amount is welcome; every contribution is acknowledged in the README's
credits, either named or anonymous.

## Custom development

Custom quotes for enterprise-specific needs; generic capabilities matured during development
are fed back into the open-source edition.

Common directions: new database or version support, custom reports and export formats,
integration with internal systems (SSO, ticketing, approval flows), private installers,
team training.

The flow: free requirements discussion first, then a quote and schedule, then development and
integration, then acceptance and delivery.
